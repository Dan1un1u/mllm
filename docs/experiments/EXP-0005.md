# EXP-0005 — Layer 14 W8A8 versus LPBQ W4A8

This diagnostic compared a per-tensor W8A8 MLP block with the existing W4G32
LPBQ W4A8 block under the same layer shape and timing protocol.

LPBQ was faster despite its W4-to-W8 expansion: 437 versus 615 microseconds at
s1 and 432 versus 619 microseconds at s32. W8A8 was approximately 40.7% and
43.3% slower respectively because its larger weight transfer was poorly
overlapped. The result confirmed that LPBQ's reduced DRAM weight traffic is
valuable and that removing W4 expansion alone is not an optimization.
