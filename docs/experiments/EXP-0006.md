# EXP-0006 — Strict LPBQ A16 versus A8 projections

The same signed W4G32 carrier and scales were paired with A16 and asymmetric A8
activations for gate, up, down, and lm_head at s1 and s32. Manifests, weight
identity, deterministic execution, and sampled host math passed.

A8 was not uniformly faster. It improved down projection by 6.29% at s1 and
7.84% at s32 and improved gate/up at s32, but regressed gate by 8.90%, up by
10.00%, and lm_head by 15.91% at s1. Optrace localized the differences to
weight-to-VTCM overlap, W4 expansion, HMX idle time, and boundary scheduling,
not to a changed LPBQ weight contract.
