# EXP-0009 — Custom U8 Softmax family

This family tested whether a custom HVX U8-to-U8 masked Softmax could remove the
native A8 prefill penalty.

## Verified sub-results

- **Placement proof:** an external HTP custom op can run on the QK-to-AV internal
  edge with VTCM-only tensors, zero DRAM, zero boundary conversion, and zero
  spill/fill.
- **E2Softmax:** 448/448 custom nodes, zero DRAM, byte-exact agreement with the
  independent integer reference, masked outputs zero, and top-1 agreement all
  passed. Full-model performance was 443.611 prefill and 37.058 decode on the
  QAIRT 2.47 protocol, with 0/100 sanity.
- **QAIRT 2.49 native study:** native remained
  `q::MaskedSoftmax_Crouton_Scratch`; lower kernel cycles did not improve
  micrograph latency or full-model prefill.
- **HVX SIMD and scale fusion:** `vdelta`, `vlut16`, `vrmpy`, and
  `vlut32` removed scalar LUT hot spots. The best full-model result was
  708.426 prefill and 41.497 decode versus the native A8 baseline's 783.838 and
  45.313, with 0/100 sanity.
- **Causal packing and QHPI:** head packing reduced accumulated custom work but
  increased accelerator cycles by 24.0% at s1 and 32.3% at s32 by creating a
  wider QK-to-Softmax-to-AV barrier.

Zero DRAM was necessary but insufficient. Multiple VTCM passes, reductions,
reciprocal dependencies, synchronization, and loss of native graph scheduling
kept the custom critical path slower. The `_Scratch` name is not evidence of a
fallback, and the closed native implementation's internal scan or precision
strategy remains unverified.
